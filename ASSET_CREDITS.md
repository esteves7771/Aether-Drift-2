# Visual asset provenance — Phase 5B

No third-party visual files were downloaded or redistributed in this milestone. The approved player/fighters and procedural environment were retained. The capital ship, its modular turrets/engines/radiators, dust, distant utility silhouettes, wreck fragments and effect meshes are generated by this project (`boss-view.ts`, `atmosphere.ts`, `art-kit.ts`, `combat-view.ts`). There are no new GLBs, texture packs, franchise spacecraft, or external runtime requests.

The following official source pages were evaluated on 2026-11-14 using their previews and published metadata. Raw geometry/materials were not inspected because no candidate was selected for integration.

## Phase 5D evaluation and original art

Rechecked the official [Kenney Space Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit) (150 files, CC0) and [Space Station Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-station-kit) (90 files, CC0) on 2026-11-14. Evaluation used published previews, categories and license metadata; no pack was downloaded or redistributed. The existing modular style and exact shared target/mount anchors favored project-authored geometry over importing an entire pack. No claims about uninspected pack topology or material quality are made.

`frontier-view.ts` authors the armored asteroid foundations, towers, launch racks, power housings, radar, maintenance deck, broken cargo/engine remnants and compact solar relays. `guardian-view.ts` authors the enormous regolith core, layered fortress armor, platforms, rotating guns, power nodes and reactor. Existing cached spacecraft, textures and pooled effects are reused. No new third-party visual files, licenses, paid assets or runtime downloads. Approved music files and their separate credits remain unchanged.
