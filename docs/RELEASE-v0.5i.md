# Aether Drift 2.0 — v0.5i

Cinematic Boss Combat 2.0, including the complete approved local Phase 5I experience.

- Orange plasma curtains, white-hot kinetic bolts, linked violet energy and telegraphed cinematic beams.
- Large 3D formations and distinct Dreadnought, Phantom and Tesser attacks, retaining existing weak points, models and gravitational-rift entrances.
- Finite boss escort fleets and coordinated dual-boss encounters beginning at Wave 20, with shared attack budgets and a dual-boss HUD.
- Three-second evasive-roll recharge and V Missile Storm: six guided missiles after 45 seconds of charge, independent of normal missile ammunition.
- All three playable ships, cockpit/chase cameras, garage, Space Voyage convoys, exploration, music and production assets remain included.

Controls: left mouse/Z fire; Space evade; M normal missile; G EMP; V Missile Storm; C camera; Escape pause.

Verification uses targeted unit tests, production Pages smoke tests on WebGPU/WebGL2 and isolated real-input boss victory scenarios. No simulation driver or encounter selector ships in production. Later Wave 40/60 pairs have unit coverage rather than full browser victory coverage. Hardware performance varies; High is expensive on software rendering.

Existing third-party attribution remains distributed with the game. No new asset packages or external services are introduced.
