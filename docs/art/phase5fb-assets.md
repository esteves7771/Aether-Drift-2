## Cockpit final prompt (built-in edit)

Use case: background-extraction / precise-object-edit. Edit target: provided approved 16:9 cockpit concept. Production game overlay PNG with real alpha transparency. Preserve exactly the physical cockpit dashboard and canopy from this image: dark gunmetal substantial lower dashboard, orange rim/accent lights, left and right tilted multifunction screen bezels, elliptical central radar bezel, narrow speed display above radar, canopy arch along top and corners. Keep matching geometry, camera perspective, composition and screen positions as closely as possible. Remove ALL the space scene through the windshield to true transparent pixels: no explosion, ships, beams, sparks, stars, nebula, target markers, score or floating text. Clean dark cockpit edges, no reflected orange explosion fire lingering along windshield. Clear the contents of every instrument screen: left and right screens empty dark glass with subtle cyan illuminated perimeter only, central radar empty dark glass with cyan elliptical perimeter but NO radar blips or ship graphic, narrow centre speed and warning screens empty dark glass with orange/cyan bezel. No text, numbers, stat bars, fake data or labels anywhere. No new cockpit design. Preserve full 16:9 layout and outer frame touching image boundaries. Transparent windshield should occupy whole upper central area down to original dashboard silhouette. Do NOT make cockpit interior transparent. No checkered pattern baked into the image. Deliver only the isolated fixed cockpit overlay with alpha.

Reference/edit target: `design-reference/cockpit.png`. Output copied unchanged from the built-in tool. Alpha and actual browser composition are checked in `tests/cinematic.spec.ts`.

## Fire atlas final prompt (built-in generation)

Use case: stylized-concept game VFX sprite asset. Generate a single seamless-layout 4 by 4 flipbook sprite sheet of one realistic cinematic spacecraft explosion, 16 equal square tiles, read left to right top to bottom, exact regular grid, no gutters, no lines, NO text or numbers. Transparent RGBA background with real alpha in every tile, not black. Each tile has generous transparent margin, all explosion pixels contained within its own tile, no tile overlaps. Explosion is isolated white-hot bright nucleus, rich orange fire, turbulent irregular flame fingers and soot with believable detailed volumetric cauliflower billows. No generic smooth glowing sphere, no circular disk, no geometric shapes, no starbursts. Camera fixed front view. Timeline continuous: first row 4 frames initial tiny white flash growing rapidly into a brilliant white/orange irregular burst; second row expansion into orange detailed branching fireball; third row fading turbulent fire and darker red glowing embers; fourth row dissipation of small ember clouds into full transparency with final tile almost empty. Consistent centered blast with physically coherent progression and no ships, stars, lasers, debris, cockpit, ground, scenery or additional objects; sparks and debris will be added by the game. High dynamic cinematic orange/white fire detail on transparent background. Square sprite sheet.

The native GLSL/WGSL shader interpolates adjacent frames, insets tile UVs and fades tile borders to avoid bleeding. This is lightweight layered sprite VFX in 3D space, not real fluid simulation. Source reference screenshots are used for visual direction only and are not redistributed as runtime assets.
