Modifications by the Aether Drift 2.0 project: FBX/DAE to static GLB conversion; missing source material assignments restored using supplied PBR maps; texture resolution reduction; geometry simplification for the heavy, saucer and stations; static source objects merged by material; NASA's Draco geometry decoded and identical gray material slots consolidated; calibration wrappers for scale/pivot/facing; game-local cannon/exhaust adapters and instance-local damage/attack feedback. Flight, weapon damage and enemy tuning are project systems, not part of the downloaded models.

NASA credit identifies the model source only. No NASA logo or insignia is used to brand or endorse the game. The source model has untextured gray materials; no photorealistic ISS textures are claimed.

The Plasticity/Boxcutter jet (mgfxer, CC BY-NC 4.0), concept ship (Aditya Voxel, CC BY 4.0) and Star Sparrow modular scene (Ebal Studios, Sketchfab Free Standard) are cataloged but not distributed or fetched by this game.

## Phase 5E additions

- **SciFi turret**, overlord.art — [original](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/scifi-turret-f73cd05c5f8046468d36d388d3fe0a5f), CC BY 4.0. Supplied scifi-turret.zip / source/Final.zip/Final.obj. Reduced from 105,652 to 15,993 triangles; retained separate base/head/gun, added Aether metallic material and effects.
- **Tesser**, Hax Lee — [original](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tesser-ee418d3d483845219875a423ea4eb695), CC BY 4.0. Supplied tesser.zip / model.dae. Retained 5,802 triangles, repaired authored Body/Parts PBR maps, capped to 1K, joined static components. Added independent gameplay weak points and engines.
- **Animated Floating Astronaut in Space Suit Loop**, LasquetiSpice — [original](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-floating-astronaut-in-space-suit-loop-e2c4b146e58141e4b87917456a9970b1), CC BY 4.0. Supplied archive of the same title / Walking astronaut.glb. Retained floating loop and skin, removed unused animation clips, welded coincident vertices, smooth normals, capped EVA maps to 512 pixels. Noncombat EVA dressing.
- **Rocks007**, Lennart Demes / ambientCG — [original](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rocks007), [CC0](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/). Official Rocks007_1K-JPG.zip; distributed only 1K Color, NormalGL, Roughness maps. Shared across seeded cratered meshes. NASA and Poly Haven catalogs evaluated; no additional rock geometry downloaded.
